## 2. Why the 7 `DELETE` Channels Must Be Removed from a Perceptual Graph

In our 2-step static box-over-box trace (`integration_trace.md`):
- At `t=0` (`static_frame=True`), the perceptual graph contains **6,877** geometric, ownership, containment, and surface edges.
- At `t=1` on the **exact same static scene**, the graph explodes to **13,893** edges because **`PERSISTENCE: 2000`, `SEQUENCE: 2000`, and `TRANSITION: 2000`** immediately hit `MAX_CANDIDATES_PER_CHANNEL = 2000`!

These 7 channels (`CO_ACTIVATION`, `TRANSITION`, `CONTRAST`, `PERIODICITY`, `PERSISTENCE`, `SEQUENCE`, `SYNCHRONY`) are **domain-blind temporal/Hebbian co-occurrence counters** inherited from symbolic MDP/puzzle environments:
1. **Zero Spatial or Retinotopic Selectivity**: They link arbitrary pattern nodes across opposite corners of the visual field solely because both patterns were active in step `t` or `t-1` -> `t`.
2. **Combinatorial $O(N^2)$ Clique Pollution**: With `~1,500` active visual pattern nodes per frame, $N^2 \approx 2.25 \times 10^6$ pairs compete for a `2,000`-edge cap, injecting arbitrary tie-break noise into subgraph isomorphism and consolidation.
3. **Category Violation**: Temporal episode transitions and action-effect sequences belong in **Tier 4 episodic/world-model memory** over bound object/event tokens — **not** as raw V1/V2/V4 edges between Gabor bars and color patches.
